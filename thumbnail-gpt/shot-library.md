# Shot library

Ready-made thumbnail concepts for Wash The Farm Animals. The user can type "shot 3" or a shot name. Each title is only used when the user asks for text.

## Shot 1: Half and half (the core shot)

- **Subject:** a big round pig filling the right half of the frame. Its left side is caked in thick brown mud; its right side is gleaming, sparkling clean pink.
- **Action:** a Roblox avatar in a bright yellow raincoat on the left blasts a power-washer jet right along the line between mud and clean. Mud chunks fly toward the camera.
- **Face:** the avatar's mouth is wide open with excitement.
- **Camera:** low angle, slight tilt.
- **Palette:** teal sky against warm mud brown and pink.
- **Title:** "WASH THE FARM!"

## Shot 2: Too big to wash

- **Subject:** a gigantic mud-covered Kaiju towering over the frame, cropped at the top edge, dripping mud.
- **Action:** one tiny Roblox avatar at the bottom aims a red fire hose up at it; the huge water jet arcs up the Kaiju's chest, knocking mud loose.
- **Face:** the avatar is shocked but determined.
- **Camera:** extreme low angle looking up.
- **Palette:** an orange sunset sky against a dark, muddy-green monster, with a bright white water jet.
- **Title:** "CAN YOU WASH IT?"
