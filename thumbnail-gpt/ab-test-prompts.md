# A/B test thumbnail prompts

Seven standalone prompts for Wash The Farm Animals. Each tests a different reason someone might click, so the A/B results tell you which hook works, not only which picture.

Each prompt is complete on its own: paste the **Base style block** and then one **Scene block** into any image model (ChatGPT, Midjourney, Ideogram, Flux and so on). Make 3-4 generations of each and keep the one that looks most like a real Studio render.

---

## How to keep them looking hand-made, not AI

The prompts already include these rules, but check the outputs against them:

1. **Generate without text, then add the title yourself** in Photopea, Canva or Photoshop. AI lettering is the biggest giveaway. Use a real chunky font (Lilita One, Luckiest Guy, Fredoka Bold, Titan One) with a white or yellow fill, a 10-14 px black stroke and a hard drop shadow.
2. **Reject any image with:** waxy or rubbery "Pixar" skin, sculpted faces, fingers on the avatars, bokeh or depth-of-field blur, god rays, haze, dreamy glow over everything, a melted or warped background, extra limbs, or a symmetrical row of characters.
3. **Real Roblox looks a bit plain.** Flat plastic, one sun, hard-edged shadows, simple shapes. If a result looks *better* than Roblox can render, it looks AI. Prefer the plainer one.
4. **Best of all:** pose the scene in Studio (the real map, real animals, Future lighting), screenshot it at 1920x1080, and use the AI only to add the water jet and mud splashes, or not at all. Use these prompts as the shot list for that.
5. **Keep the hero avatar the same in every thumbnail** (see below), so the only difference between variants is the concept.

### Hero avatar (same in every image)

A classic blocky Roblox avatar: tall and slim, small square head, flat printed smiley-style face decal, box torso, straight rectangular arms and legs with flat ends and no hands. Outfit: a bright yellow raincoat with a hood down, blue overalls, black rubber boots, a short brown moulded plastic hair piece.

## Base style block (paste first, every time)

```
A screenshot-style Roblox game thumbnail, wide 16:9 landscape, full bleed, no border.
It must look like a real in-game Roblox Studio render with Future lighting, not
concept art and not a 3D animated movie. Smooth matte plastic surfaces in flat solid
colours, simple low-detail shapes, slightly visible part edges, a single bright sun
with crisp hard-edged shadows and soft sky fill. Classic blocky Roblox avatar: tall
and slim, small square head the width of the torso, flat printed 2D face decal with a
big exaggerated expression, box torso, long straight rectangular arms and legs ending
flat with no hands or fingers. Outfit: bright yellow raincoat, blue overalls, black
boots, short brown moulded plastic hair. Animals are cute toy-like Roblox creatures
built from chunky rounded blocks with simple printed eyes, no fur, no feathers.
Mud is thick glossy chocolate-brown splatter with drips. Water is a solid white-cyan
jet with chunky droplets. Saturated cheerful colours, high contrast, one clear focal
subject filling about half the frame, simple background with one cue only.
Keep everything important inside the middle 80% of the height.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no UI, no watermark.
No depth-of-field blur, no bokeh, no lens flare, no haze, no god rays, no vignette,
no realistic skin, no fingers, no sculpted faces, no painterly or illustrated look.
```

## Thumbnail A: Half and half pig (control)

**Tests:** the core satisfying mechanic. Use this as the baseline everything else is measured against.

```
Scene: a big round pink Roblox pig fills the right half of the frame, standing on a
grey concrete wash pad. A perfectly straight line runs down the middle of the pig: its
left half is caked in thick dripping brown mud, its right half is spotless shiny pink
with small white four-point sparkle stars. The hero avatar stands on the left, leaning
forward, holding a yellow power-washer wand, blasting a thick white-cyan jet exactly
on the mud line. Chunky mud clumps fly toward the camera. The avatar's face decal has
a wide open grinning mouth. The pig's face decal looks happily surprised.
Camera: low angle looking slightly up, tilted 8 degrees.
Background: bright teal-blue sky and one red barn far behind, small and simple.
Palette: teal sky, warm mud brown, bubblegum pink.
```

**Title to add after:** WASH THE FARM! (top left)

## Thumbnail B: Too big to wash (scale shock)

**Tests:** curiosity and absurd scale. Usually the strongest for cold traffic.

```
Scene: a gigantic Roblox kaiju lizard monster, dark green with chunky block spikes,
completely covered in dripping brown mud, towers over the frame and is cut off by the
top edge. Mud pours off its arms in thick streams. At the bottom-centre, the hero
avatar looks tiny, about one tenth of the monster's height, bracing with both legs
apart and aiming a huge red fire hose upward. The white water jet arcs up and smashes
into the monster's chest, blasting a clean bright-green patch with mud exploding out.
The avatar's face decal is shocked, mouth wide open. The kaiju's face decal looks
grumpy with big round eyes.
Camera: extreme low angle from the ground looking up.
Background: plain orange sunset sky with a single white fence line at the bottom.
Palette: orange sky, dark green monster, brown mud, white water.
```

**Title to add after:** CAN YOU WASH IT?

## Thumbnail C: Rainbow reveal (rarity / greed)

**Tests:** the rare-pull hook that works for pet and brainrot games.

```
Scene: a Roblox unicorn in the centre-right, close to camera. The last chunks of
brown mud are bursting off its body in every direction, revealing a rainbow-striped
body with a soft rainbow outline glow, a golden horn and a rainbow mane. Bright white
sparkle stars and a few soap bubbles around it. The hero avatar stands on the left,
smaller, both arms thrown straight up, holding a green garden hose that is still
spraying. The avatar's face decal: huge round eyes and an O-shaped open mouth.
Camera: close up, slightly low, tilted 10 degrees.
Background: deep royal blue plain sky with a few simple white clouds, nothing else.
Palette: deep blue background so the rainbow is the brightest thing in the image.
```

**Title to add after:** RAINBOW UNICORN?! or 1 IN 1,000,000

## Thumbnail D: The mud shake (humour / reaction)

**Tests:** a funny face and chaos instead of satisfaction.

```
Scene: a black-and-white Roblox cow in the left-centre, mid-shake like a wet dog,
body twisted, with simple white curved motion lines around it. A big wave of brown mud
flies off it toward the right. The hero avatar on the right is hit full in the face,
knocked backwards and off the ground, arms flung out, a big mud splat covering half of
the face decal, the other half showing a panicked wide eye and open mouth. A sponge and
a blue bucket fly out of the avatar's reach.
Camera: high angle looking down, tilted 12 degrees.
Background: bright green grass and one white wooden fence, nothing else.
Palette: grass green, mud brown, cyan bucket and water accents.
```

**Title to add after:** DON'T LET IT SHAKE!

## Thumbnail E: Farm empire (money / idle tycoon)

**Tests:** whether players click for the money and building fantasy rather than washing.

```
Scene: the hero avatar in the foreground left, cut off at the knees, standing on a
glowing bright-green square cash pad. Green paper cash and gold coins burst upward
around them. Both arms raised, face decal overjoyed with a huge open smile. Behind,
going diagonally into the distance (not a straight row), three wooden pens with clean
glowing animals: a solid gold pig, an icy pale-cyan crystal diamond horse and a
rainbow-glowing panda, each smaller than the avatar. A red barn and a windmill at the
far right.
Camera: low angle, slight tilt.
Background: magenta-to-orange sunset sky, simple and flat.
Palette: money green against magenta sky, gold accents.
```

**Title to add after:** $1,000,000 FARM or BUILD YOUR FARM!

## Thumbnail F: Sponge vs fire hose (progression / upgrade)

**Tests:** the power-fantasy of upgrading tools.

```
Scene: split down the middle by a hard vertical line. LEFT half, desaturated and
grey-tinted: the hero avatar holding a tiny yellow sponge, barely making a dent on a
completely mud-covered pig, face decal sad and tired. RIGHT half, full bright colour:
the same hero avatar holding a huge red fire hose connected to a red fire truck,
blasting a massive white jet that rips all the mud off the same pig in one hit, mud
exploding outward, face decal grinning with determined eyebrows. A big chunky glowing
yellow arrow points from left to right across the dividing line.
Camera: slight low angle, same on both halves.
Background: simple farm grass and sky, purple tinted on the right.
Palette: grey left, purple and yellow right.
```

**Title to add after:** NOOB vs PRO (or ✗ / ✓ markers on each half)

## Thumbnail G: Before and after (classic split, minimal)

**Tests:** the simplest possible read. Very "classic Roblox", very low AI risk because it is nearly flat.

```
Scene: a single Roblox golden retriever dog, close up, filling the frame, facing the
camera. The image is divided straight down the middle. Left half: the dog is a
lumpy blob of thick brown mud with only its two printed eyes showing, a few flies as
tiny black dots, background a dull brown. Right half: the same dog perfectly clean,
shiny gold-orange plastic, white sparkle stars, a pink tongue out, a happy face decal,
a few soap bubbles, background a bright cyan sky. No avatar in this one.
Camera: straight on but slightly low, tight crop on the head and front legs.
Palette: muddy brown vs bright cyan and gold.
```

**Title to add after:** BEFORE / AFTER labels at the bottom of each half

## Running the test

| Round | Variants | What you learn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A vs B vs C | Satisfying vs scale vs rarity: the biggest question. |
| 2 | Round 1 winner vs D vs E | Whether humour or money beats it. |
| 3 | Winner vs F vs G | Whether a simpler layout beats it. |
| 4 | Winner with title vs without, then title wording | Text only after the picture is settled. |

- Change **one thing** per round. Keep the avatar, the font and the title placement the same.
- Run each round until every variant has at least a few thousand impressions before reading the click-through rate. Small samples swing wildly.
- Look at the thumbnail at phone size (about 150 px wide) before uploading. If you can't tell what's happening at that size, regenerate.
- Crop ChatGPT's 3:2 output to 1920x1080 with `crop-to-16x9.ps1`.
