# Game brief: Wash The Farm Animals

A Roblox washing game with an idle farm on top. Use this to make thumbnails that show the real game.

## The hook in one line

A filthy, mud-caked animal walks into your wash bay, you blast the mud off until it sparkles, and it joins your farm and earns you cash.

## The loop (what thumbnails can show)

1. **Wash:** a muddy animal stands in the wash bay. The player walks around it scrubbing or spraying. Mud comes off in patches, showing bright clean colour underneath. The finish is a "squeaky clean" sparkle with soap bubbles.
2. **Carry:** the player lifts the clean animal and carries it above their head to a pen.
3. **Farm:** penned animals earn cash every second onto a green pad in front of each pen. The player steps on the pad to collect it.
4. **Upgrade:** better cleaning tools wash faster and unlock rarer animals.

The best thumbnail moment is almost always the wash itself: **half muddy, half sparkling clean**, mid-spray.

## Look of the world

- A cozy, bright, sunny farm: green grass, a winding dirt road, white or wooden fences, hay bales, a red barn, a small pond, round trees, flower beds and puffy clouds.
- The wash bay is an open wash station on the farm with a mat or concrete pad, a water source and the current tool's gear parked beside it.
- Mud is thick, glossy chocolate-brown splatter and drips caked over the animal, with flying mud splashes when hit by water.
- Clean is vivid, saturated colour with white sparkle stars and soap bubbles.
- Cash is bright green with a $ sign. Amounts read like "$1.2K", "$45M", "$3B".

## Animals

All animals are Roblox-style creatures built from simple smooth plastic shapes: chunky rounded-block bodies, short sturdy legs, simple printed faces with big cute eyes. Cute and toy-like, never realistic fur or feathers.

Each species has a fixed rarity. The rarity colour is used for name tags and glows.

| Rarity | Colour | Species |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common | light grey | Chicken, Duck, Rabbit, Pig |
| Rare | blue | Sheep, Goat, Dog, Cat |
| Epic | purple | Cow, Donkey, Penguin, Fox |
| Legendary | gold yellow | Llama, Zebra, Horse, Panda, Griffon |
| Mythic | red | Lion, Hippo, Giraffe, Phoenix, Kitsune, Axolotl (limited) |
| Farm God | animated rainbow | Elephant, Unicorn, Dragon |
| Secret | black with a white outline | T-Rex, Kaiju |

Hero animals (the best thumbnail subjects):

- **Pig:** the mascot. A round pink pig, the classic first wash. Best for before/after shots.
- **Griffon:** an eagle-headed winged lion in gold and cream.
- **Phoenix:** a fiery orange and red bird with flame-shaped feathers.
- **Kitsune:** a magic white fox with several flowing tails and glowing purple markings. Its mud needs foam and soap to come off.
- **Axolotl (limited):** a pink axolotl with frilly gills. Rare, sold in limited stock.
- **Unicorn:** a white unicorn with a rainbow mane and a golden horn.
- **Dragon:** a huge green or purple dragon with wings. One of the biggest animals.
- **Elephant:** a giant grey elephant, almost too big for the wash bay.
- **T-Rex:** an enormous dinosaur covered in mud, towering over the player.
- **Kaiju:** a gigantic city-monster-style lizard, the rarest animal. The ultimate "how do I wash THAT?" shot.

Big animals are much bigger than the avatar: a Dragon, T-Rex or Kaiju makes the player look tiny.

## Mutations (variant looks)

Any animal can roll a mutation, which changes its look and boosts its income:

- **Normal:** its usual colours.
- **Gold:** shiny polished gold all over.
- **Diamond:** icy, glittering, translucent pale cyan crystal.
- **Rainbow:** shifting rainbow colours with a soft glow. The rarest and most exciting.

## Cleaning tools (weak to strong)

Each tier is visibly bigger and more powerful. Great for upgrade and progression shots.

1. **Sponge:** a yellow sponge with a soapy bucket. Scrubbing up close.
2. **Scrub Brush:** a long-handled brush with a brush caddy.
3. **Garden Hose:** a green hose on a reel. The first tool that sprays from a distance.
4. **Power Washer:** a yellow pressure-washer cart with a wand and a strong focused jet.
5. **Foam Cannon:** a foam cart blasting thick white soap foam.
6. **Fire Hose:** a huge red fire hose fed from a red fire truck. A massive jet.

## Other features worth showing

- **Mud shake:** an impatient animal shakes like a wet dog, flinging mud back onto itself and splattering nearby players.
- **Wander off:** Mythic and rarer animals walk away if the player is too slow.
- **Mud Storm:** a scheduled event where everything arrives filthy and rare animals are more likely. Dark brown storm clouds, mud rain, a dramatic sky.
- **Daily Dirty Animal:** one extra-muddy rare animal each day.
- **Index:** collecting every species in every mutation.
- **Leaderboard:** star-rated farms competing for the top spot.

## Brand

- **Title:** "WASH THE FARM ANIMALS". The short form "WASH THE FARM!" also works.
- **Mood:** joyful, satisfying, a little chaotic. Mud is funny, never gross.
- **Default palette:** sunny sky blue and grass green with bright yellow accents, or warm mud brown against teal water.
